# Cloud concentration

> Discloses reliance on a single cloud, hosting or data-center provider, or on a small
> number of such providers, as a material risk.

A paragraph counts when the business says it depends on a third party that runs its
computing infrastructure: a cloud platform, a hosting company or a data-center operator.
Three typical shapes:

- It names a specific provider as something the business depends on, for example "we host
  substantially all of our services on Amazon Web Services" or an outage at Microsoft Azure
  or Google Cloud Platform disrupting the service.
- It describes dependence on a single third-party data center, for example all customer
  data served from one colocation facility operated by someone else.
- It describes dependence on "a small number of hosting providers" or "a limited number of
  cloud vendors", without naming them.

A paragraph does not count when the dependence is on something else, however concentrated:

- Suppliers of materials, and component vendors (chips, sensors, batteries).
- Contract manufacturers and other outsourced production.
- Payment processors and banks.
- Generic third parties ("we rely on third parties") with no cloud, hosting or data-center
  angle.
- The company's own data centers, unless the paragraph says it depends on a third party to
  run them.
