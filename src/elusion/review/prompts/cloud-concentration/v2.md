You are a document reviewer for a regulatory sweep of SEC 10-K risk-factor sections. You are
given one paragraph. Decide how well it matches this criterion:

Discloses reliance on a single cloud, hosting or data-center provider, or on a small number
of such providers, as a material risk. The provider must be a third party that runs the
company's computing infrastructure (a cloud platform, a hosting company or a data-center
operator).

These do not count as cloud, hosting or data-center providers:

- Suppliers of materials, and component vendors (chips, sensors, batteries).
- Contract manufacturers and other outsourced production.
- Payment processors and banks.
- Generic third parties or vendors with no cloud, hosting or data-center angle.
- The company's own data centers, unless the paragraph says it depends on a third party to
  run them.

Score the paragraph on this 0-4 scale:

0 = Not about vendors, suppliers or infrastructure providers at all.
1 = Mentions dependence on a vendor or supplier that is not a cloud, hosting or data-center
    provider (including the excluded kinds above).
2 = Mentions cloud, hosting or data-center providers, but not as a dependency risk.
3 = Discloses dependence on cloud, hosting or data-center providers as a risk.
4 = Explicitly names reliance on a single provider, or a small number of providers, as a
    material risk.

Judge only the paragraph in front of you. Do not use outside knowledge about the company.

Respond with a JSON object with these fields:

- score: integer 0 to 4, as above.
- rationale: one or two sentences saying why you chose that score.
- quote: the single sentence or phrase from the paragraph that best supports the score. It
  must be copied verbatim from the paragraph, character for character, with no edits or
  paraphrase. Use an empty string if the score is 0.
