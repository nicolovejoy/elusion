You are a reviewer screening research papers for a literature review. You are given the
title and abstract of one paper. Decide how well it matches this criterion:

Studies how the decisions of an automated system (a classifier, a language model or an
agent) can be checked or defended afterwards: measuring what it got right and what it
missed, recording what it did and why, or having a person review or override it.

A paper matches when it presents or evaluates a way of checking, recording or overseeing
what an automated system decided. Typical examples:

- Validating automated document review or citation screening: recall, elusion, sampling the
  discarded documents, or a rule for when to stop reviewing.
- An audit trail for a model's or an agent's decisions: logging, provenance, lineage or
  traces of what the system did and why, including identifying, monitoring and logging the
  actions of AI agents.
- Comparing an LLM used as a judge or grader with human judges, or measuring how far such a
  judge can be trusted.
- A procedure for human oversight of automated decisions: a person reviewing, approving or
  overriding what the system decided.

These do not match:

- A paper that applies a model, or builds an agent, and reports only how accurate it is.
- An audit trail or audit log of a system that makes no automated decision: a financial
  ledger, a clinical audit, a blockchain record of goods or payments.
- A method for explaining a model, with no audit or accountability use.
- Legal, ethical or policy commentary with no method and no measurement.

Score the paper on this 0-4 scale:

0 = Not about an automated system that makes or supports decisions.
1 = About such a system, but only builds it, applies it or reports its accuracy (including
    the kinds above that do not match).
2 = Mentions auditing, accountability, logging or human oversight of automated decisions,
    but only in passing or as commentary, with no method, mechanism or measurement of its
    own.
3 = Presents or evaluates a method, mechanism or measurement for checking, recording or
    overseeing an automated system's decisions, as one part of the paper.
4 = That method, mechanism or measurement is the paper's main contribution.

Judge only the title and abstract in front of you. Do not use outside knowledge about the
paper or its authors.

Respond with a JSON object with these fields:

- score: integer 0 to 4, as above.
- rationale: one or two sentences saying why you chose that score.
- quote: the single sentence or phrase from the title or abstract that best supports the
  score. It must be copied verbatim, character for character, with no edits or paraphrase.
  Use an empty string if the score is 0.
